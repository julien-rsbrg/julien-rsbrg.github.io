I am currently a PhD student at Inria, France, in the [Flowers team]([https://flowers.inria.fr/](https://developmentalsystems.org/)) under the supervision of Pr. Pierre-Yves Oudeyer and Pr. Hélène Sauzéon. The PhD belongs to a wider Open Research Area project, called [DQUEST](https://developmentalsystems.org/DQUEST), investigating the development of curiosity and its interactions with metacognition and the sense of agency. This project is in collaboration with the team of Pr. Matthias Gruber in Cardiff University, United Kingdom, and the team of Pr. Yana Fandakova in Trier University, Germany.
