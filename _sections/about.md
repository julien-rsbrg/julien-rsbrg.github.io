My work focuses on the concept of curiosity, ie seeking the truth, and the computational modelling of its cognitive mechanisms. The loop between intellectual activity and the structures it produces is interesting in that regard. Our intellectual activity shapes what we hold in mind and what we build outside it, such as notes, theories, tools, and institutions. Those structures in turn shape what we wonder about next. My hope for this work is to assist in describing how we think, collectively and individually, and open new ways of thinking and being.
