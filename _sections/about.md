My work focuses on the computational modelling of mechanisms of curiosity, ie seeking the truth, and how it develops in one's life. The loop between intellectual activity and the structures it produces is interesting in that regard. Our intellectual activity shapes what we hold in mind and what we build outside it, such as notes, theories, tools, and institutions. Those structures in turn shape what we wonder about next. My hope for this work is to assist in describing how we think, collectively and individually, and open new ways of thinking and being.
